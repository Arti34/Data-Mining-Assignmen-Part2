# Research References

1. Liu, F. T., Ting, K. M., & Zhou, Z.-H. (2008), *Isolation Forest*, IEEE ICDM. DOI: 10.1109/ICDM.2008.17.
   Core idea: explicitly isolate anomalies; subsampling enables efficient detection.

2. Breunig, M. M., Kriegel, H.-P., Ng, R. T., & Sander, J. (2000), *LOF: Identifying Density-Based Local Outliers*, SIGMOD. DOI: 10.1145/335191.335388.
   Core idea: measure local deviation from neighborhood density.

3. Ruff, L. et al. (2019), *Deep Semi-Supervised Anomaly Detection*, ICLR.
   Useful context for deep anomaly detection when only limited labeled anomalies are available.

4. Du, H. et al. (2023), *AutoEncoder and LightGBM for Credit Card Fraud Detection Problems*, Symmetry 15(4), 870.
   Motivates comparing learned representations with conventional fraud modeling.

5. Wu, T. & Wang, Y. (2021), *Locally Interpretable One-Class Anomaly Detection for Credit Card Fraud Detection*.
   Highlights interpretability as an important requirement in fraud detection.

These references motivate methodology; benchmark results should not be interpreted as production guarantees.
